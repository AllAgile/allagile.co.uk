Agile coaching, Scrum Master and workshops

Appropriate for you

We have come to an understanding of lean and agile, both balance and state of flow. A place where individuals are enabled and empowered to empirically understand options and act more appropriately in the context they find themselves. All Agile Ltd. provides Scrum Master, Agile Coaching and individual coaching services. We work with you to understand and explore individual, team and organisational needs, taking you on your own unique journey.

All Agile Limited provides a range of workshops that cater to different aspects of agile coaching and Scrum Master services. These workshops are designed to help individuals and teams improve their agile practices and gain a deeper understanding of Scrum theory, practice, and reality.

These workshops are for everyone who has some involvement with Scrum’s application, including developers, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, or stakeholders. All Agile Limited also offers bespoke training workshops tailored to the specific needs of organisations.

We provide Agile Coaching, Scrum Master services and individual coaching. We also supply onsite training, organisation and facilitation of bespoke events and give meetup and conference talks to the community. We have experience and understanding of LeSS, SAFe and other scaling frameworks and have worked with teams to implement scaling as appropriate. Available workshops and facilitated events include psychology for agile teams, Story Mapping, Example Mapping, Wardley Mapping, Agile, Scrum and Kanban training, multi-team retrospectives and GV Design Sprints.
